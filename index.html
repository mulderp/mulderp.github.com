Orbits of Thought & Physical Software

thinking_on thinking started as a personal note-taking device—inspired by conversations with Marvin Minsky (The Society of Mind) and meeting Douglas Hofstadter, and grown in Munich as an open laboratory for learning JavaScript, software engineering, and hardware in public.

Like Minsky’s society of agents, this archive has no single central sun. Instead, {{ site.posts | size }} notes orbit four independent Planets—from the completed Munich tech lab to active explorations of human-machine thinking, physical software, culture, and chess.
