# Beginnings — Origins of `thinking_on thinking`

This folder collects the earliest writings, origin notes, and Marvin Minsky correspondence that inspired **thinking_on thinking** (`thinkingonthinking.com`).

## Contents

1. **[2016-01-26-marvin-minsky-passed-away.md](./2016-01-26-marvin-minsky-passed-away.md)** — *"RIP Marvin Minsky"*
   - Contains your earliest preserved writings and email exchanges with Prof. Marvin Minsky (**2003, 2004, 2006, and 2008**) on finite-state machines vs. brains, power consumption of neurons (~500 amperes of proton current!), *Music, Mind, and Meaning*, Bertrand Russell, Scarlatti/Bach/Beethoven, One Laptop Per Child (Memo 5), and *Perceptrons*/topology.
   - As noted in the post: *"That there are many emotions (like joy, fun, awe, pain, surprise) in thinking were one of the lessons prof. Minsky taught me. And, it was one of the ideas of starting my blog 'thinking_on thinking'."*

2. **[2012-12-10-An-experiment-with-Vagrant-and-Neo4J.md](./2012-12-10-An-experiment-with-Vagrant-and-Neo4J.md)** — *"An experiment with Vagrant and Neo4J"*
   - Your earliest technical blog post imported from Blogger (December 10, 2012), written after returning to Munich from the RuPy 2012 conference in Brno.

3. **[2013-01-18-new-kid-on-the-blog.md](./2013-01-18-new-kid-on-the-blog.md)** — *"New kid on the Blog"*
   - The inaugural post marking the transition from Blogger (`blogger.com`) to Git/Jekyll on GitHub Pages.

4. **[2014-03-14-essay-on-education.md](./2014-03-14-essay-on-education.md)** *(restored from Git commit `56a13683~1`)* & **[2014-03-17-on-education-innovation.md](./2014-03-17-on-education-innovation.md)** — *"On Education Innovation"*
   - Your early draft notes and published essay connecting Marvin Minsky's One-Laptop-Per-Child "cognitive map" memos with storytelling, open-source learning communities, and knowledge clusters.
